# Magical Santa — competitor benchmark (2026-11-22)

| | Dial Santa (dialsanta.app) | Call Santa Now (callsantanow.net) | **Magical Santa (us)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Format | Live AI **video** call | **Phone** call you dial (voice only; AI not disclosed) | Live AI **video** call |
| Platform | iPhone app only (iOS 17+) | Web order → dial a number | **Web, no app** — any phone/tablet |
| Price | $3.99 / 5 min · $6.99 / 12 min · $15.99 / 30 min; calls ≤ 6 min; extra characters $0.99 | Not shown on the page; per-child calls; recording upsell | **$4.99 one call · $9.99 two calls** (≤ 6 min each), shown up front |
| Personalisation | Name, wish, good deed | Name, age, wishes, good deeds (testimonials mention elf, teacher, goldfish) | Name, age, loves, celebrate, encourage, **pets/family/elf**, wish, avoid, **Santa's style** |
| Parent preview | — | — | **Exact first line + everything Santa knows, approved by the parent** |
| Social proof | none published | "4.9/5 by 12,000+ families" (unverifiable) | none yet — collect real quotes from first families |
| Trust | parental gate, no recording by default | "nothing scary, ever"; says calls are recorded *and* sells recording | no recording, no addresses/schools, honest AI disclosure, "if Santa doesn't connect, the call isn't used up" |

## Ideas taken
- Headline immediacy: **"Your child can talk to Santa tonight."** (Call Santa Now) — our calls are instant after payment.
- The small details parents love Santa to know: **pets, siblings, the family elf** → new "Pets, family & your elf" step.
- Short calls (≤ 6 min, Dial Santa) and simple one-time packs, no subscription.
- Three-step "how it works", repeated CTA, FAQ that answers "is it real / is it live / is it safe".

## Ideas deliberately not taken
- Fake or unverifiable social proof, artificial scarcity ("fills up fast").
- Hiding the price until checkout.
- Recording children's calls (privacy) — our keepsake will be a Nice List certificate built from the parent's details.
- iPhone-only app (we win Android/iPad families).
